WealthSignals Pipeline

A single-channel content pipeline, not a public product

WealthSignals Pipeline writes, narrates, and publishes short educational finance and economics videos to one YouTube channel, WealthSignals. It has one operator and one authorized Google Account. It is not a service other people sign up for.

What it does

Each run picks a topic, writes a short script, generates a spoken narration, pairs it with licensed stock footage, and publishes the finished video to the WealthSignals YouTube channel on a schedule. A human reviews the first upload before anything goes public.

Why it asks for YouTube access

The pipeline needs two things from the authorized Google Account to work:

youtube.upload
Upload and manage videos on the WealthSignals channel.

yt-analytics.readonly
Read that channel's own view and retention data, to adjust future topics.

Full detail on how that access is used and protected is in the privacy policy.

Operated as a personal project. Access can be revoked anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
